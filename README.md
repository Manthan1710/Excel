# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales Dashboard | Microsoft Excel

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to develop an interactive sales dashboard using Microsoft Excel to analyze retail sales performance, profitability, product performance, regional sales, payment preferences, and monthly sales patterns.
The dashboard provides a consolidated view of business performance through KPI cards, PivotTables, PivotCharts, and interactive slicers. It helps identify high-performing regions, popular product categories, top-selling products, and sales trends to support data-driven business decisions.

---

## 📊 Dataset Description

The dataset contains **5,000 retail sales transaction records** covering the period from October 2023 to October 2025. Each row represents an individual customer order and includes order details, customer information, product details, sales, discounts, profit, payment mode, and location.

### Dataset Columns

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| Order ID | Unique identifier for each order |
| Order Date | Date when the order was placed |
| Year | Year extracted from Order Date |
| Month | Month extracted from Order Date |
| Customer Name | Name of the customer |
| Region | Sales region (East, West, North, South) |
| City | City where the order was placed |
| Category | Main product category |
| Sub-Category | Sub-category of the product |
| Product Name | Name of the product |
| Quantity | Number of units sold |
| Unit Price | Price of one product unit |
| Sales | Total sales amount before discount |
| Discount | Discount amount applied to the order |
| Profit | Profit earned from the order |
| Payment Mode | Payment method used by the customer |
| Net Revenue | Revenue after deducting the discount |

---

## 🧮 Calculated Columns & Measures

- **Year** – Extracted from the Order Date.
- **Month** – Extracted from the Order Date.
- **Sales** – Calculated by multiplying Quantity by Unit Price.
- **Discount** – Calculated as 10% of the Sales amount.
- **Net Revenue** – Calculated by subtracting Discount from Sales.
- **Average Order Value (AOV)** – Calculated by dividing Total Sales by Total Orders.
- **Profit Margin (%)** – Calculated by dividing Total Profit by Total Sales and multiplying by 100.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### KPI Cards

- Total Orders
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Quantity Sold
- Average Order Value
- Profit Margin (%)

### Charts

1. 📈 **Monthly Sales Trend** – Shows sales performance across different months.
2. 📊 **Top 10 Products – Sales vs Profit** – Compares sales and profit generated by the top 10 products.
3. 🍩 **Payment Mode Distribution by Sales** – Shows sales contribution from different payment methods.
4. 📊 **Sales by Region** – Compares sales performance across four regions.
5. 📊 **Sales vs Profit by City** – Compares sales and profit across different cities.
6. 📊 **Sales by Product Category** – Shows sales performance across product categories.
7. 📊 **Quantity Sold by Category** – Compares the quantity of products sold in each category.

### Interactive Filters (Slicers)

- Year
- Month
- Region
- Payment Mode
- Category

These slicers allow users to filter the dashboard and explore sales performance for different time periods, regions, payment methods, and product categories.

## 🔍 Key Insights

### Overall Business Performance

- **Total Sales:** ₹59.32 Crore
- **Total Net Revenue:** ₹53.39 Crore
- **Total Profit:** ₹7.97 Crore
- **Total Orders:** 5,000
- **Total Quantity Sold:** 14,963 units
- **Average Order Value:** ₹1,18,634.22
- **Overall Profit Margin:** 13.44%

### Regional Performance

- **North** is the highest-performing region, generating approximately **₹16.03 Crore** in sales.
- **East** contributes approximately **₹15.08 Crore** in sales.
- **West** generates approximately **₹14.52 Crore** in sales.
- **South** records the lowest sales at approximately **₹13.69 Crore**.
- Sales contribution is relatively balanced across all four regions, with North contributing around 27% of total sales.

### Category Performance

- **Home Decor** generates the highest sales, contributing approximately **₹6.37 Crore**.
- **Furniture** follows with approximately **₹6.32 Crore** in sales.
- **Groceries** records the lowest sales at approximately **₹5.32 Crore**.
- Furniture generates the highest profit at approximately **₹86.93 Lakh**.
- Groceries records the lowest profit at approximately **₹70.75 Lakh**.

### Payment Mode Analysis

- **Net Banking** generates the highest sales contribution at approximately **₹12.38 Crore (20.87%)**.
- **COD** contributes approximately **₹12.11 Crore (20.42%)**.
- **Credit Card** contributes approximately **₹11.76 Crore (19.82%)**.
- **Debit Card** contributes approximately **₹11.69 Crore (19.71%)**.
- **UPI** records the lowest sales contribution at approximately **₹11.37 Crore (19.18%)**.
- The distribution indicates that sales are fairly balanced across all five payment methods.

### Monthly Sales Trend

- Sales performance varies across the different months.
- **May** records the highest combined monthly sales at approximately **₹5.61 Crore**.
- **February** records the lowest combined monthly sales at approximately **₹4.39 Crore**.
- August and July also show strong sales performance, contributing approximately ₹5.22 Crore and ₹5.19 Crore respectively.
- The Year slicer allows users to examine monthly sales patterns for individual years.

*Note: The overall month-wise figures combine the available years when no particular year is selected.*

### Product Performance

- **Headphones Accusantium** is the highest-selling product among the Top 10, generating approximately **₹9.23 Lakh** in sales.
- This product also generates approximately **₹1.42 Lakh** in profit.
- Laptop Similique and Accessories Repellendus are also among the top-performing products by sales.
- The Top 10 Products chart helps compare sales and profitability to identify important products.

### Quantity Sold by Category

- **Furniture** records the highest quantity sold, with **1,591 units**.
- **Books** follows with **1,571 units**.
- **Beauty** records the lowest quantity sold, with **1,395 units**.
- The analysis helps compare product demand across different categories.

### City Performance

- **Bangalore** is the highest-performing city by sales, generating approximately **₹3.32 Crore**.
- Guwahati and Lucknow also show strong sales performance.
- **Thiruvananthapuram** records the lowest sales at approximately **₹2.26 Crore**.
- Lucknow generates the highest city-level profit at approximately **₹45.24 Lakh**.
- The city analysis helps identify locations with stronger sales and profitability.

---

## 📝 Conclusion

The DMart Sales Dashboard provides an interactive view of overall sales performance, profitability, product performance, regional contribution, payment preferences, and monthly sales patterns.

The analysis shows that the business generated **₹59.32 Crore in total sales**, **₹53.39 Crore in net revenue**, and **₹7.97 Crore in profit**, with an overall **13.44% profit margin**.

North is the highest-performing region in terms of sales, while Home Decor generates the highest category sales. Furniture leads in profit and quantity sold. Net Banking contributes the highest sales among payment methods, and Bangalore records the highest city-level sales.

Overall, the dashboard helps identify high-performing products, regions, and categories, understand payment preferences, and monitor monthly sales performance. It provides useful business insights and supports better decision-making through interactive data visualization.
